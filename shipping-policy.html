Shipping Policy

Shipping information for Recipe Syllabus.

Delivery area

Recipe Syllabus currently delivers within India only.

Delivery time

Orders are normally delivered within 3–7 working days after dispatch. Delivery times can vary depending on the destination, courier operations, weekends, public holidays, and circumstances outside our control.

Order processing

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation. Once an order is prepared for dispatch, the courier name and tracking number may be added to your order and shown on the Track Your Order page.

Tracking

When tracking information is available, customers can use the Track Your Order link provided in the order confirmation email to check the current fulfilment status.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, city, state, and PIN code at checkout. Incorrect or incomplete address information may delay delivery.
