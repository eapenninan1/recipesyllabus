Privacy Policy

How Recipe Syllabus handles customer information.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we may collect information needed to process and deliver the order, such as your name, mobile number, email address when provided, and delivery address.

How we use the information

Customer information is used to process orders, arrange delivery, provide order and payment-related communications, provide order tracking, and respond to customer enquiries.

Payment information

Payments are processed through the payment gateway used at checkout. Recipe Syllabus does not need your full card credentials to fulfil an order. Payment-related references may be stored with the order for transaction and reconciliation purposes.

Order communications

We may send transactional messages such as payment confirmation, shipping updates, and delivery confirmation using the contact information supplied with the order.

Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the store, process payments, send transactional communications, or deliver orders. We do not need to share customer information for unrelated purposes.

Data requests

For questions about your personal information or an order, please use the Contact section of the Recipe Syllabus website.
